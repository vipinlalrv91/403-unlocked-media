The Stoic Bull | Stock Market Zero to Hero, Day 22! ETF = Exchange Traded Fund: ഒരു mutual fund, പക്ഷേ അതിന്റെ units stock exchange-ൽ share പോലെ വാങ്ങാനും വിൽക്കാനും പറ്റും. ETF = Exchange Traded Fund: a mutual fund whose units trade on the stock exchange like shares, at market price during the day. You need a demat and trading account; brokerage and other charges may apply. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks, read all scheme related documents carefully.
